# H2 Character Bible — Minimalist Film

- Character is symbolic, not a realistic human: cream round head, compact dark-grey hoodie/body, simple expressive eyes, line limbs, readable silhouette.
- No AI-photoreal skin, detailed human anatomy, random textures, or overdone neon gradients.
- Palette: charcoal #15171b, warm cream #e9e2d4, muted slate #5b6068, temptation red #b94e52, warm dawn only for the resolution.
- Every shot must have one clear visual idea and one readable action.
- Motion principles: anticipation, action, contact, follow-through, recovery. Keep all motion tied to story; no gratuitous camera zoom.
- Captions sit in a lower safe zone, with enough contrast and breathing room.
- Red temptation shadow should shrink as the character regains agency.
- Production style: text-led and sound-led. No voice-over in this initial composition.
