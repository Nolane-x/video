# Story bible — Paullus at Cannae

- Main visual protagonist: Lucius Aemilius Paullus, rendered with the Squire `consul` preset.
- Period: Second Punic War, 216 BCE. Place: Cannae, Apulia, close to the River Aufidus.
- Roman side: crimson / dark red tunics and scuta, small muted-gold eagle marks.
- Carthaginian side: blue screen palette for clarity. This is a cinematic distinction, not a claim that the historical army used a uniform blue livery.
- Paullus: short red cloak, Roman helmet, sword; chestnut horse and red cloth during the ride. Keep the same character preset and palette throughout.
- Hannibal: generic blue-toned stylized commander; no photorealistic or real-person likeness.
- World: bright, dusty Apulian plain; olive trees and distant low hills kept soft behind the characters. Use parchment for the location map and clean high-contrast top-down diagrams for manoeuvre explanation.
- Camera: push in on story turns, lateral tracking on the cavalry ride, overhead diagram on the double envelopment, brief stillness at Paullus's fall.
- Text: only short labels such as PAULLUS, HANNIBAL, CANNAE, AUFIDUS. Captions are separate, 1–3 uppercase words, bold white with dark outline; preserve bottom safe area.
- Violence: no gore. Communicate wounds through expression, posture, cloak and a dropped shield.
