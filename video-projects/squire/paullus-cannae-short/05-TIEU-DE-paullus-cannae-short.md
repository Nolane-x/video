## Description
At Cannae in 216 BCE, Roman consul Lucius Aemilius Paullus survived the cavalry fight on Rome's right. Then he rode toward the infantry at the centre — just as Hannibal's enveloping manoeuvre was closing. This short follows Polybius's account of the battle and Paullus's final moments.

The battlefield diagram is schematic; ancient troop and casualty totals are intentionally omitted.
