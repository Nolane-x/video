# Visual plan — Cannae

## Sequence map
1. **Cold open — trapped in the press** (roughly first 7%): close / scene / overhead. Open on compressed Roman ranks; the viewer cannot see the whole field yet.
2. **Return to Apulia** (roughly 7–20%): parchment geography map, Aufidus river, Cannae and Canusium. Show the supply position and campaign pressure.
3. **The numbers** (roughly 20–32%): board comparisons for infantry and cavalry; use source-attributed rounded numbers, not false precision.
4. **Two different lines** (roughly 32–48%): army wides and overhead plans. Roman infantry deep and compact; Carthaginian centre projects forward; African infantry sits on both wings.
5. **The wings fight** (roughly 48–58%): cavalry and skirmisher shots; show each wing with distinctive colour and geography.
6. **The centre gives ground** (roughly 58–72%): overhead map/plan sequence; Carthaginian centre bends inward while Roman blocks keep advancing.
7. **The enclosure closes** (roughly 72–84%): overhead pivot of Libyan wings; cavalry arcs; reaction close-ups; show loss of a clear front.
8. **Aftermath and numbers** (roughly 84–91%): quiet scene, map and sober force/casualty context.
9. **Why Rome continues** (roughly 91–100%): strategic map, commander insert, final title beat. Land the distinction between tactical victory and strategic result.

## Must-have visual facts
- The Aufidus / modern Ofanto river is a geographic anchor.
- Roman and Carthaginian force-size comparisons must state they follow Polybius and are approximate ancient figures.
- The Roman centre must visibly advance into a curved centre that yields ground.
- The two Libyan wings attack the Roman flanks; Carthaginian cavalry helps close the rear.
- Keep the final tactical diagram legible and consistent with the script.

## Sound cues
- Opening: distant battle ambience, low drone.
- Deployment: horn, drum march, restrained standards/armour.
- Cavalry: hooves gallop, horse neigh only on visible horse shots.
- Close combat: sword clash, shield bash, armour clank; sparse, no continuous sound-effect carpet.
- Aftermath: bell toll or low sting only if the visual supports it; otherwise let the music breathe.
