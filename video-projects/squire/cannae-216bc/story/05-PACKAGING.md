## Description draft
In 216 BC, Rome brought a much larger infantry force to the plain near Cannae. Hannibal answered with a formation that gave ground in the centre, drew the Roman mass forward, and exposed it to attacks from both flanks. This animated history film follows Polybius's account while separating ancient estimates from modern certainty.

Force strengths and casualty figures are ancient estimates; the tactical sequence is an explanatory reconstruction. The Squire characters are stylized visual shorthand, not authenticated reconstructions of every ancient uniform.
