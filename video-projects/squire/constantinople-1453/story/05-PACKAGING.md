# Packaging

**Title:** 53 Days to the End of an Empire

**Primary thumbnail concept:** a towering battered wall on the right, a giant Ottoman bombard firing on the left, the Golden Horn and an Ottoman galley visible through smoke. Two-word title: “53 DAYS”. High contrast, no tiny labels.

**Description opening:** A city believed to be impregnable faced 53 days of artillery, naval manoeuvres, engineering and repeated assaults. This film follows the siege of Constantinople from 6 April to 29 May 1453.

**Chapters:** The final night; A city between two seas; Mehmed prepares; The guns arrive; Four ships break through; Ships over the hill; Fifty-three days of attrition; The last assault; The city after the fall.

**Editorial note:** This is a historically grounded animated reconstruction. The composite narrator is fictional. Disputed details are identified in the narration and are not presented as direct eyewitness footage.
