# Ain Jalut — H2 Squire long-form production

**Working title:** The Day the Mongols Were Stopped at Ain Jalut  
**Target:** 16:9 · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · English long-form film · over ten minutes  
**Engine:** H2 Squire 2D/vector animation on GitHub Actions

## Production
1. Read the research ledger and visual bible; author a standalone script.
2. Select an eligible English narrator from the live VoiceStudio manifest.
3. Render and watermark the narration; preserve its original PCM master and provenance.
4. Align speech to line and word timing; draft voice-timed Squire shots.
5. Author map, army, mounted combat, close-up, item, comparison and overhead tactical scenes.
6. Build visual contact sheets and a 45-second moving preview before the full render.
7. Mix the voice with a restrained score and recorded Foley candidates; retain exact source and rights metadata.
8. Render 1080p, build thumbnail, captions sidecars, QA evidence and SHA-256 checksums.

## Historical handling
- Medieval source accounts differ on the exact battle sequence and the role of a feigned retreat.
- Exact army and casualty numbers are not treated as settled facts.
- Ain Jalut checked the Mongol advance into Egypt; it did not end Mongol power or later campaigns.
- Mamluk and Mongol characters are stylised composites, not authenticated uniforms or likenesses.

## Review gate
A green Actions run proves technical checks, not artistic sign-off. The complete video and soundtrack must still be watched/listened to at normal speed before public release.
