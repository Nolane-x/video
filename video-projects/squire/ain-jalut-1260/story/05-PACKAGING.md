## YouTube description
On 3 September 1260, a Mamluk army met the Mongol force commanded by Kitbuqa at Ayn Jalut. The battle checked the Mongol advance into Egypt — but was it won by a perfect feigned retreat, or by a harder combination of timing, reserves, terrain and leadership? This animated history film follows the campaign from Baghdad and Syria to the battlefield, while separating medieval claims from what historians can say with confidence.

The tactical diagrams are explanatory reconstructions. Army strengths and casualty totals are not presented as exact modern counts. The feigned-retreat account is disputed. Squire's character designs are stylised visual shorthand, not authenticated portraits or uniforms.

Chapters will be finalised from the verified VoiceStudio word timeline after rendering. Sources: Sean P. Webber, U.S. Army Command and General Staff College (2023); Carl F. Petry, *The Mamluk Sultanate*; Charles J. Halperin (2000).
