# THE FIRST HOUR — character and art bible

- Theme: procrastination and compulsive scrolling; the first hour is reclaimed through one small, repeatable action.
- Runtime target: 172 seconds; 29 shots, 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps; English on-screen text; no voice-over.
- Accent: amber. Use it only for the temptation voice, HUD reset, headband during the recovery beat, key light accents and end card.
- Hero: cream round-headed young man; use hoodie for late-night / relapse beats, plain hero before change, amber headband only after he starts acting.
- Voice: taller black shadow with amber rim, crescent eyes and grin; close and large during relapse, farther/smaller as the hero recovers.
- Palette: charcoal/plum shadows, cold moonlight and phone blue during avoidance, warm practical amber light during the turn and reconnection.
- Camera: alternate black title card, room wide, object inserts, close face, city tracking, desk medium and warm two-shot. Use gentle push-ins only to reinforce visible action.
- Contact/action checks: alarm hand reaches clock; hand touches/places phone; pen tip touches notebook; fingers type at laptop; both people face each other at cafe. If template guess doesn't show contact, revise shot args before final.
- Audio: generated piano sad -> dark drone -> full silence at the collapse -> hope pad -> rising rhythm -> warm resolve. Keep SFX subordinate to captions.
- QA gate: contact sheet, short moving preview with sound, action/contact samples, ffprobe, EBU R128 and SHA-256 before delivery.
