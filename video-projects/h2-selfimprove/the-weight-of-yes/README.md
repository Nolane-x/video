# THE WEIGHT OF YES

A 143-second, 29-shot H2 animated short about people-pleasing, boundaries and reciprocal care.

- Delivery checklist and checksums: `../../../video-library/the-weight-of-yes/QA.md`

## Production gate
This is not marked complete when the script merely exists. Completion requires a successful native H2 render, MP4 stream checks, EBU R128 loudness/true-peak checks, valid proof clips, contact sheets, and a human visual review. A CI pass is technical evidence, not artistic sign-off.

## Locked creative decisions
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps; English on-screen text; no voice-over.
- Keep the request scenes physical and human; do not turn this into another phone-addiction story.
- The decisive “NO. Not today.” beat must have space, then hold long enough to show the other person's non-punitive response.
- The story resolves through a concrete submitted project and reciprocal care, not a magical montage.
