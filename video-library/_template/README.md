# New film template

Copy this folder to `video-library/<new-project-slug>/`, then replace every placeholder in `manifest.json` and `QA.md`.

Required before “final”:
- source commit/branch and exact renderer;
- actual master + preview URLs and storage status;
- duration/resolution/FPS/codecs and audio measurements;
- source/asset provenance and license notes;
- full contact sheet and motion-proof clip;
- QA findings with P0/P1/P2 severity;
- known limitations and next steps.

Do not mark an artifact archived until the permanent URL works and checksum/metadata are recorded.
