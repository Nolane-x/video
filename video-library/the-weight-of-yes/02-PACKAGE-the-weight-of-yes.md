## Description
He was everyone's first call. Every favor seemed small, every yes sounded harmless, and everyone else's day became easier. His own life was the thing he kept postponing.

Then one ordinary request met one honest answer.

**THE WEIGHT OF YES** is a short animated story about people-pleasing, personal boundaries, and learning that kindness can be real without requiring you to disappear.

You can care about people and still protect your time. A boundary is not a betrayal.
