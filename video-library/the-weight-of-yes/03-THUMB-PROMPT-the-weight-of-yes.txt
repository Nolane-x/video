THE WEIGHT OF YES — THUMBNAIL DIRECTIONS
Format: 16:9, designed for a small mobile preview, charcoal/plum shadows, a restrained amber accent, consistent H2 stick-figure character. No logos, no fake UI, no extra fingers/limbs, no microscopic text.

T01 — TOO MUCH
Hero in the foreground, shoulders compressed, holding an oversized but believable stack of folders against his chest. Three classmates are visible behind him, each casually extending one more paper. The hero is the only face losing its warm light. Strong diagonal composition; warm light remains in the far background, cool slate/teal over the hero. Large empty area at upper left for 2–3 words: “TOO MUCH”. Expression must read as exhaustion, not comedy.

T02 — THE WORD
Close, cinematic two-shot: hero standing upright for the first time, one hand gently raised in a calm boundary gesture; a friend pauses and looks at him, surprised but not angry. Split lighting: cool classroom shadow on the left, warm amber light finding the hero’s face and hand. Keep both faces large and readable. Text, if used: “SAY NO”.

T03 — THE EMPTY SPACE
A desk with a completed personal project in a small pool of warm light. In the background, friends work together rather than waiting for the hero to rescue them. Hero is seated with a relaxed posture and one open hand free. Moody charcoal negative space, subtle amber edge light, hopeful rather than triumphant. Optional text: “YOUR TIME, TOO”.

Preferred: T02. It contains the story’s actual turning point and communicates the subject at thumbnail scale without turning the friend into an antagonist.
