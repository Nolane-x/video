# Illustrated History Animation Director

Use for an original illustrated/cartoon history film. This is separate from H2 Squire's vector military style and from footage-led historical documentary.

## Reference study
- Probe/hash the exact source. Map its entire duration with systematic samples; inspect dense motion samples around important actions/cuts.
- Record precise timecodes and scope. Separate observation from interpretation; never claim to have inspected all frames if only sampling was performed.
- Do not copy protected characters, compositions, scripts, narration, music or SFX.

## Visual direction
- Use expressive, consistent character rigs, but invest in specific environments, props, clothing, historical artwork and spatial depth.
- Alternate establishing wides, medium action, close reactions, inserts, overhead geography, portrait cards, timelines, data graphics and human/social scenes.
- Use maps as progressive information graphics: reveal terrain/context, tokens, borders/routes, then consequences.
- Use recurring motifs to connect distant sections of a long film.
- Every shot has a narrative purpose; avoid repeated faces, generic weapon inserts, same army rows and map filler.

## Motion architecture
Every motion-led shot defines independent channels for actor, prop, environment, camera and graphics. At least two channels should move independently. For physical action, show anticipation → action → contact → reaction → follow-through → recovery. The camera must not substitute for character/object action.
Build reusable walk/march/run/idle/gesture/group cycles; horse gait and rider bounce; weapon draw/aim/release/contact; bow/arrow/target; ship/rowing/boarding; rain/smoke/fire/water/flags/crowds; and map/infographic reveals. Vary timing and cycle phases to avoid synchronized repetition.

## Engine selection
Select H2 rigs, Remotion, Motion Canvas, Krita, Blender and FFmpeg by shot needs. Motion Canvas is a candidate for vector/info motion, not an assumed installed dependency. Benchmark a small shot, validate dependency/license and render a moving proof before scaling.

## Audio and QA
Use audio-library/SOURCES.json and audio-library/README.md first. Route selection/acquisition through historical-audio-library-curator. Use auditioned licensed recordings before procedural sound. Keep sound stems/cue sheets separate; align Foley to contact and perspective. Inspect a full moving sample with audio and maintain a timecoded defect log. Render success is not artistic approval.
