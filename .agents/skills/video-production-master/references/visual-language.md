# Visual language and art direction

## Lessons from user-provided references
“The Words You Never Forgot” changes intentionally between locker room, close-up, mirror/shadow, bedroom, intrusive words, school/bus-stop, information insert and recovery. “What If You Were a Mentalist?” varies location and scale with multiple characters, prop inserts, reactions, over-shoulder and wide shots.

The rule is **simple character + rich set + purposeful cuts + readable action**. Don't over-complicate the character to create production value; stage the world and action carefully.

## Character
- Symbolic stick figure: near-black circular head, pale rim, white eyes/mouth/brows, slim dark limbs, simple hands/shoes, distinct shirt color.
- Keep proportions consistent with a character bible and reusable rig.
- Color shifts should have story meaning (rose for the loop, muted ochre for recovery in ONE MORE SCROLL).
- Emotion should read at delivery scale through eyes, brows, head angle, shoulders and hand pose.
- No photorealistic humans, fake skin detail or glossy AI gradients unless explicitly requested.

## World and light
Give every set story-specific objects: doors, windows, lockers, desks, notebook, cup, stairs, bag, shelves, bus stop, mirror, plants and lamps. Layer foreground/subject/background; match parallax to camera. Warm amber practical lights contrast plum/charcoal shadows; bloom is local and motivated. Ground the character and props with contact shadows and coherent perspective.

## Shot grammar
Choose deliberately among wide establishing, medium action, prop insert, reaction close-up, two-shot/over-shoulder, silhouette/held beat and resolution. A cut should change information, action, space, feeling or perspective. Avoid dozens of near-identical centered shots.

## Camera and typography
Each camera move should reveal, follow, isolate, compare, interrupt, emphasize impact or create breathing room. Do not apply generic zoom to every shot. Avoid unintended head/foot crops. Keep captions short, legible and away from faces/hands/props. HUD only when story-justified; do not copy the IQ dial from the user's example.

## Rights
Use reference footage for analysis only. Don't reuse/tracing their frames, scripts, characters or music.
