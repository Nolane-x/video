# Visual Reference Study: Ifverse Tales × Day One Films

## Why the previous render missed the target

The previous Remotion prototype was too empty and flat: generic gradient backgrounds, small simplified characters, little architectural detail, and a large bottom text banner. It looked like a basic motion-graphics demo rather than a story scene. The target reference supplied by the user instead combines simple dark-faced stick-figure characters with a carefully staged urban environment, warm windows, architectural edges, plants, stairs, shadows, and restrained cinematic focus.

## What to learn from each reference

### Ifverse Tales
Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@IfverseTales

Example: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MroF1XmHOZw

Use as reference for:
- instantly readable symbolic stick-figure characters
- one clear premise, action, obstacle, and consequence per sequence
- varied story situations instead of repeating one character pose
- clear visual cause and effect; make an action understandable before explaining it with text
- environments and props that help stage the scenario

Do not assume the channel uses any particular software; its pipeline is not established by these observations.

### Day One Films
Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@dayonefilmsofficial

Use as reference for:
- a consistent protagonist across a short emotional arc
- dark, restrained, cinematic mood
- a destructive loop, moment of recognition, and a final choice
- high-contrast light and shadow that reinforce the emotion
- concise shots and a strong final visual beat

The Day One reference is about story and atmosphere; do not copy its character, compositions, scripts, or music.

## Combined direction for H2

**Character:** simple, repeatable stick-figure/mascot design. Near-black round head with light outline, sparse readable face, colored shirt, dark thin limbs with a pale rim. Proportions are fixed in a character sheet. No realistic human body and no generated pseudo-photoreal person.

**World:** richer than the character, not emptier. Build apartments, rooms, doors, stairs, desks, windows, plants and street props as layered vectors/2.5D assets. Add depth with perspective, light pools, contact shadows and selective background softness.

**Light:** cool plum/charcoal shadows plus warm amber practicals. Muted accent colors on clothes and a restrained story accent (e.g. a dark shadow figure). Avoid constant neon, overexposed glow, AI-looking gradients, and unrelated magical effects.

**Camera:** motivated wide shots, lateral tracking, insert shots, reaction shots, slow pushes and held frames. The camera should serve the action; do not use a generic zoom to simulate movement.

**Acting:** every action must include anticipation, a readable main movement, contact, follow-through and recovery. Tie hands to objects and feet to floors/steps. Keep eye/head direction aligned with the object or person of attention.

**Text:** small film subtitle/HUD treatment. No oversized title card covering the character for most of the shot. In-world HUD is allowed only when justified by the story; the example's IQ meter is not to be copied literally.

**Audio:** the H2 workflow is text/music/SFX-led by default. Keep music and sound effects on separate tracks and include intentional silence where the emotional beat needs it. Do not introduce machine voice-over as a shortcut.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] A contact sheet shows different camera sizes and compositions, not eight identical centered shots.
- [ ] Character remains consistent in scale, silhouette, outline thickness, and face layout.
- [ ] Background architecture is readable and compositionally balanced.
- [ ] Windows/lamps are practical light sources, not full-frame glow effects.
- [ ] Character feet touch the ground/steps in every action pose.
- [ ] Phone/pen/props meet the correct hand or surface.
- [ ] The shadow element has a story reason and recedes as agency increases.
- [ ] Subtitle/HUD doesn't cover the acting.
- [ ] The MP4 has been inspected as a moving sequence, not just validated as a file.
