# Historical Cinematic Documentary Playbook

This playbook adds a footage-led, cinematic route for sweeping historical films. It does not replace the established H2 Squire/vector-animation route or the repository's stick-figure house style.

## Why a separate route exists

A historical documentary such as a full history of the samurai needs more than code-drawn characters and tactical diagrams. The picture can combine:
- historical photographs, museum objects, paintings, manuscripts and maps;
- original 3D environments, architecture, terrain, armor, weapons, ships, castles and formations;
- 2.5D projection/parallax and camera passes through layered evidence;
- animated maps, chronology, troop paths, family lines and annotated documents;
- controlled reenactment, silhouette action, cloth/armor motion and physically plausible contact;
- footage and visual plates selected from the existing 50 GB resource pool.

Keep source evidence visible when it matters. A museum object insert, a map reveal, a wide terrain plate and a close physical action answer different questions. Do not put every sentence over the same face, battlefield row or diagram.

## 3-pass edit

**Paper edit:** research claims, source notes, estimates, contested interpretations, script beats and chapter turns.

**Radio edit:** record and review narration, arrange music, room tone and actual recorded effects; cut the narration and sound first. Mark holds, breath, silence, emotional pivots and impact beats.

**Picture edit:** choose evidence, locations, action, 3D, 2.5D and typography against the audio edit. Visualize each key idea once in the strongest form; avoid duplicate coverage that adds no information.

## Motion standard

Every shot must specify:
- scale, height, angle, lens intention and focus;
- camera path, direction, speed, ease and destination;
- subject and prop choreography;
- physical contact and resulting reaction;
- foreground/midground/background depth;
- light, atmosphere, materials and colour;
- edit reason, sound beat and exact duration.

Use purposeful dolly/track/orbit/crane/aerial/pan/tilt/handheld/reveal/rack-focus/match-cut/whip/speed-ramp/hold variations. Build actor and prop animation, not only camera moves. Test frame-seek determinism; repeated seeks must produce identical frames.

## Sound standard

The default source is a real recording or a correctly licensed musical recording/sample. Search the existing audio bundles first; select by sound function, material, environment, period feel, mic perspective, duration and dynamics. Keep independent stems for voice, score, ambience, Foley, impacts, crowds/animals and transitions. Code may synchronize and process these stems, but should not create the entire soundtrack from oscillators.

## Resource policy

The main Build 76 release already has distinct audio bundles and multiple footage/photo/3D bundles. Search the manifest before any new model generation or download. Per-record rights and attribution remain mandatory. See audio-library/README.md and audio-library/SOURCES.json.

## Quality bar

A preview must prove a real camera move, at least one complex actor/prop action, layered depth, varied shot scales, motivated transitions and a soundscape made from selected recordings. A successful render or contact sheet is not artistic approval. The whole moving film and full mix must be watched/listened to at normal speed before release.
