## Core visual grammar to carry forward

1. Character-led action: readable silhouettes, gaze, expression, weight shifts, pose changes and prop contact.
2. Environment-led storytelling: castles, villages, roads, ports, ships, markets, workshops, training spaces, political rooms and landscapes.
3. Progressive information graphics: map context → actors/tokens → route/movement → consequence; timelines, portrait/name cards, ranks, diagrams and myth/fact comparisons.
4. Varied editorial scale: establishing wide, group medium, close reaction, prop insert, profile/over-shoulder, overhead geography and graphic tableau.
5. Layered motion: actor, prop, environment, camera and graphic channels must be planned separately. At least two should move independently in a motion-led shot.
6. Narrative alternation: combat is balanced with domestic, political, social, economic, travel, craft, aftermath and legacy beats.
7. Motif callbacks: return to a character, mount, symbol or camera idea only when it pays off story meaning.
